Kaapool Safety Guidelines

Safety and trust are the foundation of Kaapool. We connect members traveling along the same routes to share costs, reduce emissions, and build a dependable carpooling community. Please review our safety guidelines and standards below.

1. Platform Trust & Member Verification

Every trip on Kaapool is supported by robust verification systems and community accountability. We require all members to verify their identities before joining or reserving rides.

  • Mandatory Government ID Verification: Every member must verify their mobile phone number via OTP and submit a government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Driving License, or Passport) prior to offering or booking rides.
  • Women-Only Carpool Options: Female drivers can publish rides exclusively for women, and female passengers can filter search results to book female-only trips.
  • Transparent 2-Way Rating System: Passengers and drivers rate and review each other after every completed ride to maintain high standards of courtesy, punctuality, and respect.
  • Data Privacy & Contact Masking: Personal phone numbers and email addresses remain protected. All pre-trip coordination and messaging take place securely within the Kaapool application.

2. Essential Safety Tips for Passengers

As a passenger, following these simple guidelines ensures a smooth, comfortable, and secure journey from pickup to arrival.

  • Review Driver Profiles Prior to Booking: Check the driver’s overall star rating, member reviews, total completed rides, and vehicle specifications before confirming your seat.
  • Verify Car & Driver Details at Pickup: Always confirm that the driver’s name, car model, color, and license plate number match the booking details displayed on your screen before entering the vehicle.
  • Share Your Trip Status: Send your booking summary and live trip updates to trusted family members or friends prior to departure.
  • Keep Bookings & Payments Official: Complete all seat reservations and transactions inside the Kaapool platform to ensure clear receipts, cancellation coverage, and trip tracking.

Passenger Tip: Arrive at your agreed pickup location 5 minutes early and communicate any unexpected delays through Kaapool in-app messaging.

3. Essential Safety Tips for Drivers

Offering empty seats in your car comes with the responsibility of ensuring a safe, clean, and reliable driving experience for your co-passengers.

  • Maintain Valid Vehicle & Driving Documents: Keep your driving license, vehicle registration (RC), pollution certificate (PUC), and insurance policies fully active and up to date.
  • Confirm Passenger Identities at Pickup: Greet your passengers at the designated pickup point and verify their names against your active passenger manifest.
  • Adhere to Safe Driving Standards: Always follow traffic regulations, observe speed limits, avoid mobile phone distractions while driving, and refrain from driving when tired or fatigued.
  • Provide a Clean & Comfortable Environment: Maintain a clean, well-serviced, non-smoking vehicle cabin to ensure pleasant travel for everyone on board.

4. Reporting Issues & Zero Tolerance Policy

Kaapool enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against harassment, dangerous driving, discrimination, or fraudulent activity. Any member found violating our community standards faces immediate, permanent account suspension.

  • Instant In-App Reporting: Report any unprofessional behavior, no-shows, or safety concerns directly through your dashboard or booking details.
  • 24/7 Safety Support Team: Our dedicated safety team investigates all reports promptly to take appropriate action.

Emergency Situations: In case of an immediate emergency or safety threat on the road, contact local police emergency services (112 / 100) immediately.
